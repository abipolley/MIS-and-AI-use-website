From this assignment, I learned about MIS, AI, and security. MIS stands for Management Information Systems, and it is a useful tool for businesses that links them to the world of technology that they opperate in today. MIS is crucial for helping businesses stay organized and manage themselves effectively, combining multiple disciplines of business into one unified system. I also learned about the pros and cons of AI usage, and how business and academic settings alike are working through the proper balance of AI in both fields. AI is a useful tool when used in the correct context, but it has the power to be misused if given the opportunity. Finally, i leanred about security and how worms such as WannaCry spread quickly because of computers being connected through networks. Cybersecurity is incredibly important to consider when looking at MIS and AI because they are both technology-based. Both platforms are at risk of compromise, and thus a focus on security is crucial for optimal performance for anyone using MIS and AI.
