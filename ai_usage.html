Correct AI Usage

The correct use of AI is that it be used as a helpful tool to automate certain tasks. AI should be used as a supplement and not a replacement for human work. An important thing to consider when using AI properly is providing the chosen AI with correct context and parameters, treating it similar to an assistant. AI should not be used in situations containing sensitive information or without proper oversight, as it is not immune to user error.

Incorrect AI Usage

Plagiarism
Students directly copy work generated by AI and present it as their own without disclosing AI use.

Cheating on Exams or Assignments
Students use AI to answer questions on exams or assignments and do not know the information themselves.

Generating Fake Citations
Students use AI to generate sources or citations for assignments without verifying if they are applicable or even real.

My personal opinion on AI is that it is a useful tool for better understanding difficult concepts to improve performance. AI should not replace critical thinking or problem solving, it should supplement these processes. Incorrect AI use is dangerous in academic settings because of the ease of access students have to AI platforms. There is an abundance of temptation for students to incorrectly use AI on assignments, but the easier path is not always the better one.
