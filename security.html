Malware and Cyber Threats

WannaCry

An example of a cyber attack is the ransomware attack WannaCry, which spread across over 150 countries and impacted hundreds of thousands of computers. This attack began on May 12, 2017 and only lasted a few hours. This malware was a worm which automatically spreads to computers in a network; WannaCry used a vulnerability exploit called "Eternal Blue" which worked on unpatched versions of Microsoft Windows. This attack is important in cybersecurity history because it showed how quickly worms spread and the negative impacts of having unpatched software.
